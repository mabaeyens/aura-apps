The sky, where you'd actually see it

One layout, shared by the iPhone and the Watch. Behind it, a light that moves the way the real sun does — rising on the left in the east, overhead at midday, setting on the right in the west, and gone below the horizon at night. Aura already computes the true sunrise and sunset for your town, so the glow sits where the sun actually is. This is the weather app I always wished I had.

The same cards in the same order, morning to night. Only the light changes — and it changes because the hour changed, not for decoration.

Even the clouds obey the hour

The sun's position is a layer that's always there — on clear days and cloudy ones alike. Cloud only draws a veil over it: thicker cloud, softer light. So the same "nuboso" never looks the same at 9:00 and at 19:00 — it glows cool from the east in the morning and warm from the west at dusk.

What I can draw in code — no illustrator

The scenery kit

Every piece below is a flat SwiftUI vector, so it's parametric: it re-tints with the sky, its river catches the sun's colour, and its shadows lean away from the sun. All of it scales to the Watch. The last tile is the one thing code can't do alone.

Why this is buildable now

Most of it already exists

The redesign is mostly layout on top of colour science that's already in Palette.swift. Here's the split.

Have Time-of-day gradient

timeGradient already drifts dawn → zenith → dusk → night. It becomes the full-bleed background instead of just the header.

Have Condition palette & temp ramp

skyGradient, skyAccent and the continuous temperature colour drive every card and number, unchanged.

Have Real sun times

SolarTimes gives true sunrise/sunset per location — so the sun's screen position is the actual sun, computed once per view, not on a timer.

Build Sun / moon glow

A radial glow placed by daylight progress → x, altitude → y. At night, a dimmer moon glow plus a scatter of stars. Small and self-contained.

Build Shared cards in AuraKit

Hero / Hourly / Daily / Sun·Wind / Alert / Bulletin, composed once. Kills today's phone↔watch drift for good.

Build Flat vector scenery

Hills, mountain, tree, sun-lit river — layered SwiftUI shapes tinted to the sky. Skyline and isometric city are optional add-ons.
